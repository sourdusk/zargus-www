Request data deletion

You can request deletion of your Zargus Cloud data at any time. This removes your account identifier, API-key hash, registered device tokens, and any purchase-token reference from our servers. There is nothing else to delete — Zargus stores no other data about you, and all data on your device (server credentials, cached problems, settings) is removed by uninstalling the app.

Email dusk@sourk9.com from any email address with the word Zargus in the subject. Include the account ID shown in the app under Settings → Zargus Cloud (it looks like za0123456789abcdef) if you have one.

We complete deletion requests within 7 days and reply to confirm. If you never enabled Zargus Cloud, you have no data with us and no request is needed.
